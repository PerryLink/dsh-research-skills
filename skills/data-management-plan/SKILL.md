# Data management plan

## Rules

- A DMP says where data lives, who can touch it, how long it is kept and what happens after the grant.
- Personal data and open sharing cannot both be claimed without a lawful basis.
- An ethics checklist pass is not ethics approval.

## Steps

1. `dmp` for the ten-section scaffold; fill each section with the user's facts, not boilerplate.
2. `irb` for the ethics application checklist (18 starter items, risk level and safeguards).
3. `dataset_card` for each dataset; run the PII-like column check before proposing open sharing.
4. `research_report` the completed plan and the open questions; seal it.
5. Keep the repository/licence/DOI fields consistent with the funder's policy.

## Output

- Completed sections with explicit unknowns.
- Consistency findings (personal data vs open sharing, retention vs policy, backup vs repository).
- Ethics checklist gaps and the risk level they imply.

## Never

- Do not invent a repository, licence or DOI.
- Do not claim ethics approval.
